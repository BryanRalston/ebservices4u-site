Questions

Handyman & Home Repairs FAQ.

Have another question? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

Is my job too small?

Handyman work is a core part of what we do, so small jobs are welcome. Grouping a few items together makes the most of a visit.

Can you work through a punch list?

Yes. Send us the list in the estimate form and we will talk through what is involved.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Handyman jobs and small to medium size home projects, including bathrooms, carpentry, painting and drywall.

How do I get an estimate?

Send the estimate form with your list or project details. We will follow up to talk it through.
