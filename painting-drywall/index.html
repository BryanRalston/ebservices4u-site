  • Interior painting

Questions

Painting & Drywall FAQ.

Have another question? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

Can you patch drywall and then paint it?

Yes. We repair and patch the drywall, prep the surface and paint so the repair blends in.

Do you create patterned accent walls?

Yes. Feature walls with geometric trim patterns and bold colors are one of the projects we have done. See the photo on this page.

Can you paint just one room?

Yes. A single room, a single wall or a whole floor are all welcome.

How do I get an estimate?

Send the estimate form with the rooms or repairs involved. Photos help, and we will follow up to talk through the job.
