Questions

Frequently asked.

Don't see your question? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Our specialties are bathroom remodeling, basement finishing, kitchen remodeling and media walls. We also take on carpentry and trim, painting and drywall, and handyman repairs.

How do the AI previews work?

Evvon photographs your actual space, then uses AI to create photorealistic previews of what it will look like when the work is done. You can review the design and talk through changes before any work begins. Previews are for visualization, and the final result may vary slightly.

Do you install TVs, AV or electronics?

No. Our media walls are carpentry and finish work: built-ins, shelving, paneling, trim and paint. We build the wall; your preferred AV or electronics installer handles the technology.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. EBServices LLC is licensed and insured.

Who will be working on my project?

EBServices is owner-operated. Evvon Boxill leads every project personally, from the first consultation to the final walkthrough.

Is my job too small?

Handyman work is a core part of what we do, so smaller jobs are welcome. Tell us what is on your list in the estimate form.

Do you do full bathroom remodels?

Yes. We handle bathroom projects from a single vanity or fixture swap up to a full refresh with new showers, tubs, lighting and finishes. See the projects above for examples.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (571) 302-1240, fill out the estimate form, or email Ebservices4U@outlook.com. We'll follow up to talk through the job.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are free and there is no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

EBServices is based in Leesburg and serves homeowners across Loudoun County and Northern Virginia.

How long has EBServices been in business?

Over two years. EBServices LLC was formed in Virginia in 2023, built on years of hands-on experience delivering custom solutions for homes.
