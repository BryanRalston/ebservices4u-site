What kinds of jobs do you take on?

Handyman jobs and small to medium size projects. That includes bathroom remodeling (vanities, showers, tubs and powder rooms), interior doors, closet and pantry shelving, accent walls and general repairs and installs.

Is my job too small?

Handyman work is a core part of what we do, so smaller jobs are welcome. Tell us what is on your list in the estimate form.

Do you do full bathroom remodels?

Yes. We handle bathroom projects from a single vanity or fixture swap up to a full refresh with new showers, tubs, lighting and finishes. See the projects above for examples.

How do I get an estimate?

Fill out the estimate form with a few details about your project, or email Ebservices4U@outlook.com. We'll follow up to talk through the job.
