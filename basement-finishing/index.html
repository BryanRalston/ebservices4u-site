Questions

Basement Finishing FAQ.

Have another question? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

What can my basement become?

Family rooms, guest suites, home offices, gyms, rec rooms and more. We plan the layout around how you want to use the space.

Can you add a bathroom in the basement?

Yes. A basement bathroom can be part of the project. Tell us about it when you request an estimate and we will talk through what is involved.

Can you build a media wall downstairs?

Yes. We build the wall itself: built-ins, shelving, cabinetry, paneling, trim and paint. Your preferred AV or electronics installer handles the technology.

How do I get started?

Call (571) 302-1240 or send the estimate form with a few details about your basement. Evvon will follow up to talk it through.

Can I see what it will look like before you start?

Yes. Evvon photographs your actual space and uses AI to create photorealistic previews of the finished room, so you can see the design before work begins. Previews are for visualization, and the final result may vary slightly.

What areas do you serve?

EBServices LLC is based in Leesburg and serves homeowners across Loudoun County and Northern Virginia.
