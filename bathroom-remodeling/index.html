Questions

Bathroom Remodeling FAQ.

Have another question? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

Can you replace just the vanity?

Yes. A single vanity, sink or faucet replacement is a common job for us, and so is a full bathroom refresh.

Do you remodel small powder rooms?

Yes. Powder rooms are a great place to make a big visual change in a small space, with a new vanity, mirror, lighting or an accent wall.

Can I pick my own fixtures and finishes?

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind when you request an estimate, and we will talk through the options for your space.

How do I get started?

Call (571) 302-1240 or send the estimate form with a few details about your bathroom. We will follow up to talk through the project.

Can I see what it will look like before you start?

Yes. Evvon photographs your actual space and uses AI to create photorealistic previews of the finished room, so you can see the design before work begins. Previews are for visualization, and the final result may vary slightly.

What areas do you serve?

EBServices LLC is based in Leesburg and serves homeowners across Loudoun County and Northern Virginia.
